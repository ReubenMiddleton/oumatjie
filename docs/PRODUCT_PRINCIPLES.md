# Product principles

Oumatjie is for people who may have limited vision, dexterity, memory, confidence, literacy, or familiarity with technology. Age must not be treated as a single disability or a single user profile.

## Interaction rules

- Every important action has a visible text label.
- No essential action relies on swiping, long-pressing, or remembering a hidden gesture.
- Primary touch targets are at least 64dp high; 48dp remains the absolute minimum.
- The interface supports Android font scaling and display scaling without clipping.
- Language is concrete: “Move to Trash” instead of “Delete”; “Back to your mail” instead of an arrow alone.
- The app shows one primary decision at a time and keeps navigation shallow.
- Destructive or external actions are explained and reversible where possible.
- Unknown senders, external links, and unusual requests receive calm, factual warnings.
- Accessibility includes TalkBack semantics, keyboard navigation, contrast, and reduced motion—not only large text.

## Privacy rules

- Use OAuth; never collect Gmail passwords.
- Request permissions just in time and explain why they are needed.
- Keep email processing on the device unless a feature genuinely requires a server.
- Download attachments only when requested and remove temporary copies automatically.
- Never store PDF passwords by default.
- AI features must be optional, explicit, and unable to send mail or follow links without confirmation.

## Research approach

Build a participant panel rather than assuming one person's needs represent everyone. Include differences in vision, motor control, language, education, Android experience, device size, and confidence. Observe task completion and confusion; do not rely only on preference surveys.
